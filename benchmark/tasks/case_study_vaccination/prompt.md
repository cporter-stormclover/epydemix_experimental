A COVID-like epidemic (R0 = 2.5, latent period 4 days, infectious period 5
days) starts in Italy on 2026-10-01 and runs to 2027-03-31, with all four
contact layers. A leaky vaccine with 80% efficacy is given from the start.
Compare four scenarios: no vaccination, and a low, a moderate and a high
vaccination rate. Use 50 simulations per scenario.

Save them as outputs/no_vaccination.epx, outputs/low.epx,
outputs/moderate.epx and outputs/high.epx. Report the attack rate of each,
the ranking of the campaigns, and whether that ranking is sensitive to the
values you chose for low, moderate and high.
